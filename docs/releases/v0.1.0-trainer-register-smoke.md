## What changed

- Trainer vocabulary answers now carry a register: `formal`, `informal`, or `neutral`.
- Trainer cards show a compact register badge: `formalnie`, `nieformalnie`, or `neutralnie`.
- Correctness feedback separates grammar/translation correctness from style/register fit.
- A formally targeted card can accept a grammatically valid informal answer while still explaining the register mismatch.
